How It Began

From Istanbul's Kitchens
to Prague's Streets

"The best meal I ever had was at my grandmother's table in Gaziantep. I wanted everyone to feel that."

Divan opened its doors with one obsession: recreating the honest, comforting food of a Turkish household. Not the tourist-facing version, not an adapted Western interpretation — the real thing. The börek your mother makes at 6am. The lentil soup that fixes everything.

Prague was the natural home. A city that understands craft, values tradition, and welcomes the world with open arms. Since opening, Divan has become a gathering point for the Turkish community and for anyone who wants to understand what homemade truly means.

Every dish on our menu carries a memory. Every ingredient is chosen with the same care a grandmother chooses what goes into her pot.
